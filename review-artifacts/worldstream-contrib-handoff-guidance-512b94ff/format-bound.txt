The exact reviewed formatter uses a 1,200-byte char array and a single format for both presentations. The compact form substitutes the literal <substitute data.bundle value> for the local path. Counting its fixed C strings and bounding each dynamic argument by the actual storage (79 commit characters, 64 for each of two hash insertions, 23 for each of two size insertions, 48 for the lane) gives at most 886 bytes including the terminating NUL. The long-path capture also exercised this form through the ordinary CLI.
